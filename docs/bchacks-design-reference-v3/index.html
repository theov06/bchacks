About BCHacks

Build something real in one weekend.

BCHacks is UBC Okanagan's student hackathon. For one weekend in November, students team up to turn an idea into something that works: an app, a game, a gadget, a tool.

Bring an idea or find one there. Mentors help you get unstuck, and on Sunday you demo what you built.

What is a hackathon?+

A weekend where you team up to build a project from scratch, learn new tools along the way, and show what you made at the end.

How much does it cost?+

Nothing. BCHacks is free to attend.

Who can attend?+

[CONFIRM: UBC Okanagan students only, or students from any school?] Beginners are welcome.

Do I need a team or an idea?+

No. Come solo and team up at the event, or bring friends. Teams of up to [4].

I've never coded. Can I still come?+

Yes. Beginners are welcome, and mentors are there to help.
